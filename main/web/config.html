433 MHz transmitter
--
Same waveforms as firmware 2.3 (rc-switch 2.6.4): NOVY protocol 12 at 320 µs, fan protocol 1 at 248 µs with 3 frames per command. NOVY commands send 10 frames per repeat until the first fan command after boot, 3 from then on, exactly as 2.3 did.
Fan cycle positions
Next code index (0–7) per fan button; every button has its own cycle, starting at 0 after boot.

One Label=URL per line, at most 12. Label: 1–24 letters, digits, spaces, _ . or -. URL: http:// or https://, at most 96 characters. The menu stays hidden until the list holds another device.
